Agent Builder in Microsoft 365 Copilot

Build an agent your team will actually use

A hands-on course for Logan's AI Champions. Find a question your team asks every day, build an agent in Agent Builder that answers it, and keep council information safe along the way.

Start lesson 1 View the lessons

You'll learn to

  • Understand how AI actually works, and why it gets things wrong
  • Find a good agent idea for your team
  • Word prompts and instructions to get the result you want
  • Build, test and share an agent in Agent Builder
  • Choose and prepare knowledge files
  • Know where Claude fits alongside Copilot
  • Spot PII (personally identifiable information), risky documents and unauthorised add-ons, and know where your data goes
  • Test an agent the way someone would try to break it

What you'll walk away with

  • An agent idea for your team
  • A prompt ready to paste into Agent Builder
  • Instructions written for your agent
  • A test kit and pre-share checklist
  • A one-page plan for your agent
  • A certificate to show you've done it

9 short lessons, about 95 minutes. Go at your own pace. Your progress saves in this browser.

How an agent gets from idea to your team

  1. YouSpot a good ideaA question your team asks every day, answered in a few documents.
  2. YouBuild it in Agent BuilderInstructions, knowledge, capabilities off unless needed.
  3. YouTest it in Try itReal questions, break-it prompts, the pre-share checklist.
  4. AI teamApprovalThe AI team checks it against the five habits.
  5. YouShare with your teamSpecific people or groups, as Can chat. Org-wide sharing off.
