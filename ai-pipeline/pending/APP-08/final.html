    How it works. Pick a project, choose what to write (meeting minutes, an email or a status report) and start from your rough notes, an email you received or the project log. AI drafts it in plain English and lists the decisions, risks and actions it found, which you can add to the project's decision and risk log with one click. The log flags gaps such as risks with no owner or overdue actions, and you can fix them in place or email the owner to chase them. You edit, check and copy the draft, or open it in Outlook. Nothing is sent from here.
