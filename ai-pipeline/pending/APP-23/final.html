    How it works. Ask a question in plain words and the assistant answers only from the fleet library, with numbered sources you can open. Pick your role so the answer fits your job. Each answer links to the learning module that covers it. The Learn tab shows each crew member's pathway for their role and walks them through short modules with quick quizzes, and Toolbox talk drafts a five minute talk for a supervisor. If the library can't answer, flag it so the knowledge owner can fill the gap.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: Vue and Bootstrap front end on Azure App Service, an Azure Function App for model calls, Azure AI Search over the approved fleet documents in SharePoint or Blob Storage, SQL or Cosmos DB for quiz results and flagged gaps, Entra ID sign-in.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement or Microsoft Foundry.
    • Privacy: names, email addresses, phone numbers and vehicle registrations are swapped for placeholders before a question goes to AI. Quiz results stay in the page.
    • All procedures, clauses and quiz questions here are samples. The real ones must come from City Fleet's approved documents, checked by the document owners.
    • Still needed for production: the approved document set and owners, a link to the council's learning system for training records, a test plan with an AI evaluation set, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
