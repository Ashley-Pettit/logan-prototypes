Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. The jobs, streets, zones, costs, contractor return and grate photos are made up for this demo. Officers check every programme and photo rating before crews are sent.

This week's settings

Very high risk jobs are always included, even over budget.

Crews available

Sample backlog of 20 jobs across four made-up zones. Changes update the programme straight away.

How it works. Each job gets a priority score from its safety risk (likelihood times consequence), road type, customer requests, age and nearby schools or bus stops. The page fills the weekly budget in score order, always keeping very high risk jobs, then groups each crew's jobs by zone and nearest next stop to cut travel. AI reviews the programme, explains the order in plain English and suggests swaps you can apply. The contractor data tab checks a contractor's defect survey return against the contract data rules (reference, date, street, zone, defect, size, photo and GPS), flags likely repeats of backlog jobs, drafts a query for missing details and adds clean records to the backlog. AI can read messy rows and free-text notes, after names, phone numbers, emails and house numbers are swapped for placeholders. The grate photo review asks AI to rate how blocked a grate is and turns the result into a backlog job. Without AI, the rules still rank, sequence and check the contractor return, and officers rate photos by hand.
Prototype handover notes
  • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for scoring, sequencing and model calls, SQL for the job backlog and programmes, Blob Storage for grate photos, and ArcGIS for real locations and routing.
  • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider for explanations and photo rating, through the council's enterprise agreement or Microsoft Foundry.
  • Contractor returns: agree the required fields with the contract manager, take returns through an upload or API, and keep the redaction of names and contact details before any AI call.
  • Privacy: road jobs hold locations, not people. Customer request details stay in the council's request system and only counts come across. Grate photos must be cropped or blurred so no people, faces or number plates are sent to AI.
  • The ranking rules are transparent and officers can pin or defer any job. AI only explains and suggests; a supervisor approves the programme before it goes to crews.
  • Still needed for production: the real works backlog and asset data, agreed scoring weights with Road Maintenance, a photo evaluation set with officer ratings, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB, PGG and Business System Owner approvals.
