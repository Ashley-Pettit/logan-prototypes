Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. All businesses, people, licence numbers and addresses are made up. Every draft must be checked and issued by an authorised officer.

Recent inspections

Sample inspections, made up for this demo.

    How it works. Pick an inspection, choose which findings belong in the notice, then let AI draft each item in plain English with the action needed and a date to comply. Legislative references come from the page's own library, never from AI. The officer edits, checks and downloads the draft. Nothing is sent to the business from here.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls, Cosmos DB or SQL for drafts, Pathway or the inspection app as the data source.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement.
    • Privacy: only the officer's findings, category and risk level are sent to AI. Business names, licence numbers, addresses and officer names stay in the page.
    • Legislative references and the review rights wording here are samples. They must be checked against the approved LCC improvement notice template by Health, Environment and Sustainability before use.
    • Still needed for production: approved template, test plan with an AI evaluation set, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
