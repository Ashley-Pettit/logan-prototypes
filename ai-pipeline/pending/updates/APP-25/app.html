How it works. The page finds the clauses in the IT Standards Portal that best match your question. AI then gives a plain-English answer using only those clauses, with links straight to each source clause. Check a proposal compares a short description of a tool or project against the standards and lists what meets, what needs action and what does not. Without AI, you still get the matching clauses.
Prototype handover notes
  • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for search and model calls, SharePoint or Blob Storage as the source for the IT Standards Portal content, with links to the live standard pages.
  • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement.
  • Privacy: only the question, the chosen role and matching clauses are sent to AI. Names, email addresses and phone numbers in questions and proposals are swapped for placeholders first. Staff should not enter personal information.
  • Every standard, clause and contact in this demo is made up. Real content must come from the published IT Standards Portal, with each standard's owner signing off the Q&A test set.
  • Still needed for production: connection to the real portal, a test plan with an AI evaluation set (questions with known answers and pass rates), Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
