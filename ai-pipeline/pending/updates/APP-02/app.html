Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. The road segments, condition scores, defects, traffic counts and costs are made up for this demo. The model is simplified and supports, not replaces, engineering judgement.

Road segments

Sample sealed road segments with condition surveys, made up for this demo. PCI is a 0 to 100 condition score.

    How it works. Each segment has condition survey results (PCI, 0 to 100) over time and defect results (cracking, rutting and potholes) from the latest survey. The page starts from a network deterioration curve adjusted for traffic, subgrade, drainage and surface, then calibrates it to each segment's own survey results. It predicts when the segment will hit the reseal, resurface and rehabilitation triggers, prices each option, reads the defects with the environment data to suggest the likely cause, and learns which factors (drainage, flooding, subgrade, heavy vehicles and surface) speed up deterioration across the network. The Pavement design tab works out design traffic and granular pavement thickness. AI explains the results in plain English and answers questions, quoting the survey data it used. Without AI, the page shows a template explanation built from the same numbers.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), a Python Azure Function App for model calibration and AI calls, Azure SQL for segment, survey and model results, and data sourced from the Data Warehouse and the council's pavement and asset management systems.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement or Microsoft Foundry.
    • Privacy: road condition, traffic and environment data hold no personal information. Customer requests or defect reports with resident details must be stripped of personal details before they reach the model.
    • The deterioration model is a simple power curve calibrated by least squares, for discussion only. Production needs the council's adopted model and treatment rules, checked by Transport Planning and Assets engineers against Austroads guidance.
    • Still needed for production: real survey history and traffic counts, adopted unit rates, a test plan with an evaluation set (segments with known outcomes), Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB, PGG and Business System Owner approvals.
