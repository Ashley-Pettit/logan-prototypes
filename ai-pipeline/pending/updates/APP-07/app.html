Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. The sites, briefs, unit rates and costs are made up for this demo. Concepts are early ideas for discussion, not designs. An engineer must check every option against council standards.

Site briefs

Sample Transport Planning and Assets briefs, made up for this demo.

    How it works. Pick a site brief and adjust the constraints: budget, trees to keep, drainage and anything else the designer should know. AI suggests two or three concept options as design settings, such as path width, alignment, bay layout or pole spacing. The page then draws each concept, works out rough quantities and a cost range from sample unit rates, and checks it against sample design rules. Compare the options, mark a preferred one, then copy a short concept note to send or download the full note and sketches. Without AI, the page builds three template options you can still compare.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls and the cost engine, SQL for unit rates and saved concepts, and ArcGIS for real site boundaries, trees, drainage and services.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement or Microsoft Foundry.
    • AI only chooses settings from fixed lists. Quantities, costs and design checks are worked out by the page, so the AI cannot invent a price or skip a check.
    • Privacy: briefs are about places, not people. The page masks emails and phone numbers in notes before anything is sent to AI.
    • Still needed for production: the council's current unit rates, its design standards as checkable rules, GIS layers for each site, a test plan with an AI evaluation set (briefs with expected sensible options), Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB, PGG and Business System Owner approvals.
