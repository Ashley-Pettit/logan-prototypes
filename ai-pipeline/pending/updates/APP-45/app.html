Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. Every event, venue, organiser, past event and permit rule is made up. Drafts must be checked by a venues officer before they go to an organiser.

Upcoming event bookings

Sample bookings, made up for this demo.

    How it works. Pick an event booking and check the brief. The page finds lessons from similar past events and the hazards that apply. AI then drafts the event plan, run sheet and risk register, scored on a 5 by 5 risk matrix. The officer edits, ticks off permits, runs the checks and copies or downloads the result. Nothing is sent to the organiser from here.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls, SQL or Cosmos DB for briefs, plans and risk registers, and the council's venue booking system as the source of bookings.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement.
    • Privacy: organiser names, phone numbers and email addresses stay in the page. Only the event details, activities, hazard library and past event lessons are sent to AI, with contact details swapped for placeholders.
    • The risk matrix, hazard library, permit rules and past event lessons here are samples. They must be replaced with the council's approved event risk matrix, event plan template and debrief records, signed off by Venues and Events and Work Health and Safety.
    • Still needed for production: approved templates, a test plan with an AI evaluation set, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
    Based on idea 335129, Event Planning and Risk Assessment Assistant (Community Venues). Rapid AI prototype for discussion, not a council system.
