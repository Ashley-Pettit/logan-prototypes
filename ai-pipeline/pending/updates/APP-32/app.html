Documents to review

Sample documents, made up for this demo.

    How it works. Pick a document and the checklist it must meet. AI reads the document against every checklist item and says whether each one is met, partly met or missing, with a quote as evidence and a suggested fix. Blockers stop submission; gaps should be fixed. You then work through the fix list, or accept a risk with a reason, and download the review. Without AI, a quick keyword check runs instead.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls, SharePoint or Blob Storage for documents, Cosmos DB for checklists and review records.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement.
    • Privacy: email addresses, phone numbers and names in the sample contact lines are swapped for placeholders before text goes to AI. Real documents may hold more personal information, so production needs proper redaction and a records check.
    • The checklists here are samples. Real ones must come from the owners: Governance for council reports, the grants team for grant rules, Development Assessment for approval conditions, and the policy framework owner for policies.
    • Still needed for production: checklist owners, a test plan with an AI evaluation set (documents with known gaps), Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
