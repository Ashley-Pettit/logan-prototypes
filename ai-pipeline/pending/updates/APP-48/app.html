Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. The listings, groups, businesses, depots and amounts are made up for this demo. AI drafts listings and suggests matches; a waste officer checks safety and approves every hand-over.

Surplus materials

Sample listings, made up for this demo.

    How it works. A council team or local business describes surplus material in plain words. AI turns it into a clear listing with category, quantity, condition and safety notes, then suggests who on the reuse register is most likely to use it and why. A waste officer confirms the safety check, offers the material to a group, and records the collection so the tonnes kept out of landfill add up. Without AI, the page builds a template listing and matches by keywords and category.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap) on Azure App Service, an Azure Function App for model calls and matching, Azure SQL for listings, the reuse register and hand-overs, and Blob Storage for photos.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement or Microsoft Foundry. A public-facing version would use Haiku only, with no access to internal systems.
    • Privacy: the reuse register holds organisation names and a role contact only. Personal names, phone numbers and emails are masked before anything is sent to AI, and the description box warns people not to type them.
    • Safety: every listing needs a reuse safety check (asbestos, contamination, treated timber, test and tag) confirmed by a person before it is published. AI safety notes are prompts to check, not clearances.
    • Still needed for production: the real reuse register and consent to be contacted, waste and resource recovery approval of the safety checklist, a test plan with an AI evaluation set (material descriptions with expected categories and matches), Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB, PGG and Business System Owner approvals.
