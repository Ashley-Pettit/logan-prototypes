Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. The contracts, businesses, venues, amounts and dates are made up for this demo. AI drafts a review for the contract manager to check. It is not legal advice.

Contracts and leases

Sample Venues and Events contracts, made up for this demo.

    How it works. Pick a contract or lease. The review lists every obligation with who owns it and when it is due, flags risky clauses against the council's contract checklist, and links each item to the clause it came from. Ask questions about one contract or all of them and every answer quotes its clauses. Tick obligations off as they are done, draft a reminder to the other party about what they owe, and copy or export the key dates. Without AI, the page shows the prepared sample reviews and searches the clauses instead.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls, the council's contract register and SharePoint contract library as the sources, Azure AI Search over clause text, and SQL for obligations, owners and reminders.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement or Microsoft Foundry.
    • Privacy: contracts mostly hold business details, but leases and hire agreements can name individuals. The page masks emails and phone numbers before anything is sent to AI, and the paste box asks users to remove personal details first.
    • Every obligation, risk and answer must cite a real clause, and the page drops any it cannot match. Contract managers confirm the review before relying on it. It does not replace Legal Services advice.
    • Still needed for production: the real contract register and documents, a test plan with an AI evaluation set (contracts with known obligations and dates), reminder emails, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB, PGG and Business System Owner approvals.
