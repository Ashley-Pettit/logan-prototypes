Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. Every project, parcel, owner, valuation, legal advice and file note is made up. AI answers and reports must be checked by a property officer before use.

Acquisition projects

Sample projects, made up for this demo.

    How it works. Pick an acquisition project to see every parcel, its stage, valuation, offer and next action. Ask a question and AI answers from the past acquisition files, quoting the file it used. Then draft a status report for the project manager, the executive or a councillor briefing, built from the parcel data. Nothing is sent to owners from here.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls, Azure AI Search over past acquisition files in SharePoint or the records system, and SQL for the parcel register.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement.
    • Privacy: owner names, property addresses and lot numbers stay in the page. AI only sees parcel numbers, stages, amounts and file notes with names swapped for placeholders.
    • Legal advice, valuations and procedures here are samples. Real answers must come from the council's approved acquisition procedure, delegations and legal advice, and stay subject to legal professional privilege controls.
    • Still needed for production: a controlled library of past files, a test plan with an AI evaluation set, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
    Based on ideas 332474 (Acquisition Project Copilot Agent) and 332475 (Land Acquisition Status Reporting Automation), Corporate Property. Rapid AI prototype for discussion, not a council system.
