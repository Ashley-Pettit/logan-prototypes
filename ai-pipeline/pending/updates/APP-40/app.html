Approved procedures

Sample Venues and Events procedures, made up for this demo.

    How it works. Pick an approved procedure, choose who the training is for and which formats you need. AI rewrites the procedure as a quick guide, a checklist and a short quiz, and tags every point with the procedure step it came from. The page then checks every step is covered and that no new numbers crept in. The procedure owner approves before anything goes to staff. Without AI, the page builds plain template versions you can edit.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls, SharePoint as the source of approved procedures, and Blob Storage or SharePoint for approved training packs.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement.
    • Privacy: procedures are internal process documents and should not hold personal details. The paste box warns users to remove names, phone numbers and emails before generating, and the page masks emails and phone numbers before anything is sent to AI.
    • Approval: AI drafts are never published to staff from here. The procedure owner must approve each pack; production would log approvals and link packs to the procedure version so they are refreshed when it changes.
    • Still needed for production: real procedure library and owners, a test plan with an AI evaluation set (procedures with expected key points), Dev, Test and Prod environments, LMS export format if needed, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
