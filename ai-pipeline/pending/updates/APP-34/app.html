Venue spreadsheets

Sample month-end sheets, made up for this demo.

    How it works. Pick a venue spreadsheet or load your own Excel or CSV file. The page checks every line against budget, looks for unusual months and steady trends, compares with last year and checks the sheet adds up. AI then explains each finding in plain English, suggests likely causes to check and writes a short summary for the manager. The officer marks each finding and copies the summary or the questions for budget owners. Nothing is sent to anyone from here.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls and the checks, SQL for review history, with month-end figures from the council's finance system or the Data Warehouse instead of hand-made spreadsheets.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement.
    • Privacy: only account names, numbers and the findings are sent to AI. The preparer's name and any extra columns stay in the page, and emails, phone numbers and titled names (such as Ms Citizen) in account names are swapped for placeholders.
    • Excel files are read in the browser with the SheetJS library, loaded only when someone uploads an Excel file. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
    • The thresholds (10% and $2,000 by default) and severity rules are samples. Corporate Support and Finance need to agree the real ones.
    • Still needed for production: agreed rules, a test plan with an AI evaluation set of past month-end packs, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
