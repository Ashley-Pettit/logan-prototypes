Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. The change requests, people, CAB rules, dates and catalogue here are made up. The change manager makes the final call on every change.
How it works. Pick a change request or describe a new one. The page applies the sample CAB rule book to say whether it needs CAB, Emergency CAB or no CAB, with the rule behind each reason, the approvals needed and the next CAB date. A word check flags risks in the description that the form missed. AI, when it is on, reads the description more closely, explains the result in plain English and drafts the CAB submission. The change manager decides.
Prototype handover notes
  • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls and the rules engine, SQL for change checks and the rule book, and the council's IT service management system as the source of change records.
  • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement.
  • Privacy: requester names, emails and phone numbers are swapped for placeholders before anything goes to AI. Change records should not hold personal information about residents.
  • The CAB rule book, standard change catalogue, meeting day, lead times and freeze dates here are samples. They must be replaced with the council's approved change management procedure, owned by the change manager.
  • Still needed for production: approved rule book, test plan with an AI evaluation set of past changes, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
