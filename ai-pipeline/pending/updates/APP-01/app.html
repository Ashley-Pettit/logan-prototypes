Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. Every road, crash record, traffic count, request and person here is made up. Scores, weights and crash reduction figures are illustrative, not the council's adopted method. Results must be checked by a traffic engineer.

Ranked road segments

Twelve sample segments with five years of made-up crash records.

    How it works. The page scores every segment from four factors: crash history (weighted by severity, per kilometre per year), speed, pedestrian exposure and road layout. You can change the weights and see the ranking move. It also ranks road safety issues across the network, such as pedestrians struck or run off road crashes, by severity. AI explains each segment's risk from its data, suggests treatments from the treatment list and writes a briefing. The page does the scoring, so every number can be traced. AI explains it and never changes it.
    Prototype handover notes
