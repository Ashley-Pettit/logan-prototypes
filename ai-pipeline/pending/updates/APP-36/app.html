IIP assessment backlog

Sample requests, made up for this demo.

    How it works. Pick a request from the backlog and check the project details. AI drafts a finding for each privacy principle, quoting the part of the proposal it relied on, and suggests actions. The page checks each quote against the proposal and works out the risk rating from the findings. The privacy officer confirms or changes every finding, then copies the advice memo and questions for the project owner. Nothing is sent from here.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for model calls, SQL or Cosmos DB for requests and assessments, and SharePoint or the records system for the final advice.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement.
    • Privacy: project owner names and email addresses stay in the page and are swapped for placeholders before anything goes to AI. Proposals should describe data types, not contain real personal information.
    • The principle summaries, the council policy check, risk scoring and memo wording are samples. They must be replaced with wording approved by Corporate Governance and checked against the Information Privacy Act 2009 (Qld) and the council's privacy policy.
    • Still needed for production: approved templates, a test plan with an AI evaluation set of past assessments, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
