Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. Venues, fees, hirers and enquiries are made up for this demo and are not current council fees or policy. Every reply must be checked by venue staff before it is sent.

Hire enquiries

Sample enquiries, made up for this demo. Pick one to draft a reply.

    How it works. Pick a customer enquiry, or paste a new one and the page reads the venue, times and extras from it. The page works out an indicative fee from the sample fee schedule and finds the venue document sections that match each question. AI then lists each question in the enquiry, answers it from those sections with numbered sources, and drafts a reply for staff to check and send. Staff can also ask quick questions on the Ask tab. Without AI, you get a template reply and the best matching sections.
    Prototype handover notes
    • In the LCC stack for production: HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end (move to Vue and Bootstrap), Azure Function App for search and model calls, SharePoint or Blob Storage for venue documents, and the booking system as the source for venues, rooms and fees.
    • Needs an ICT-DA exception: Claude (Anthropic) as the AI provider, through the council's enterprise agreement. As a staff-facing tool it can use the standard model; a customer-facing version would need Haiku only and no access to internal systems.
    • Privacy: hirer names, emails and phone numbers are swapped for placeholders before anything is sent to AI and put back in the page afterwards.
    • All fees, bonds, surcharges and rules here are made-up samples. Real answers must come from the adopted fees and charges register and the approved venue hire conditions.
    • Still needed for production: document owners and review dates, a test plan with an AI evaluation set of real enquiry types, Dev, Test and Prod environments, and ICT-DA, CAB and business owner approvals.
