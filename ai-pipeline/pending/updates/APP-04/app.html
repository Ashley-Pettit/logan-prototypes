Rapid AI prototypeSample data only. The RFQ, suppliers, people and prices are made up. Personal details are swapped for placeholders before anything is sent to AI. AI suggests, the panel decides.

Four sample submissions are already scored with a rule-based first pass. Adjust any score in the matrix, check the quoted evidence, then draft evaluation comments, a recommendation summary and clarification requests to suppliers. The second tab drafts a specification for a new RFQ.

Probity:AI suggestions are advisory only. The evaluation panel scores and makes every decision. Conflicts of interest are declared and managed outside this tool, before evaluation starts.
